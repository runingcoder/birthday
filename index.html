18–23°

The take-off angle of good long jumpers. Textbook physics says 45°, but one step can't turn a sprint into that much lift, so the best jumps trade angle for speed.

Eccentrics

Your own prescription in August 2022, for the knee. Slow eccentric loading is a standard rehab tool for knee tendon pain, and tendons adapt over months, not weeks.
